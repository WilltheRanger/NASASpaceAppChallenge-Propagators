# Anchor submission summary draft

Anchor asks a simple question: when a satellite observes fires at a different time of day, how much does the record change? Our web application combines NASA FIRMS MODIS and VIIRS observations with INPE reference counts to explore fire activity across six regions. It presents satellite look-time drift, a harmonized burning calendar, and comparisons with a fixed-clock reference. We check aggregate Aqua counts against INPE, compare two MODIS observation times, and report uncertainty and tests that did not pass. The goal is to help users distinguish changes in observations from changes in fire activity, rather than treating every trend as proof of a physical change. Anchor makes those assumptions and limits visible alongside the data.
