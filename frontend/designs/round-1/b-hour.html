Brazil's fire count is read off a satellite that now looks at 14:12, not 13:18.

Fires burn harder in the afternoon, so a later look counts more of them. Held to a fixed clock, the Amazon's 2024→2025 change is a fall of 29.5%, a little steeper than the official 26.8%.

The clock moved

Aqua's look time over the Amazon held at 13:12 until 2020, then slid later every year. VIIRS held its early-afternoon slot. Terra slid the other way.
