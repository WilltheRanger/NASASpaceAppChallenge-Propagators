Why

Aqua's clock has drifted

Brazil's official fire record (INPE) still uses Aqua as its reference satellite.
Its named successor, S-NPP VIIRS, was switched off on Nov 2, 2026.
Method

VIIRS becomes the fixed clock

1Baseline · 2012–2020
R = Aqua ÷ VIIRS
per region × calendar month, before the drift
2Fixed-clock count
fixed = VIIRS × R
in Aqua units: what Aqua would count at its old clock
3Clock audit · 2021 onward
L = Aqua ÷ fixed
gap = L − 1: how far an Aqua-based record has drifted
